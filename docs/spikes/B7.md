# B7 — Ultra page structure for the dashboard, floating widgets, course cards and nav (evidence)

The orchestrator made one read-only pass on 2026-10-15 in a Chrome **without Glaze**, signed in to a real Ultra school
(about 113 requests, almost all Ultra's own page traffic). It recorded structure only (tags, classes, computed layout,
link paths with ids masked) and no values. Other schools may differ: degrade quietly when an anchor is missing.

## Courses page (`/ultra/course`): why the card due list overlaps
- `div.courses-panel > div.base-courses > div.course-list > section#course-columns-allTerms.course-columns.multi-column-view`
  (`display: block`) `> div.course-org-list` (`display: grid`).
- The grid's rows alternate a term heading (about 47px) and a term's group of cards. Computed rows on the account:
  `46.79px 300px 46.79px 276px 46.79px 276px`, sized by content (no inline style).
- `div.favorites-group.term-_N_N[.last-item]` (one row, 300px) > `bb-base-course-card` (inline) >
  `article#course-list-course-_N_N.element-card.course-element-card` with **`display: flex; height: 284px`**
  (a fixed height from Ultra's own cross-origin stylesheet, so its rule couldn't be read), `align-items: start`,
  `overflow: visible`, `position: relative`.
- The article's children are `div.course-banner` (128px) and `div.element-details.summary` (154px, `flex: 1 1 auto`).
- **The cause:** Glaze's `.gz-ct` due list is placed after `.element-details` inside the fixed-height article, so it
  spills out of the card. With `overflow: visible` it draws over the next row (the next term heading and its cards).
  Glaze's own card height settings (`data-gz-card-height`) also fix the height, which leaves tall, empty cards.
  The owner's screenshot shows both.

## Left nav (this school: the newer MUI drawer, no `#base_tools`)
- `div.MuiPaper-root.MuiDrawer-paper` is **200px wide**.
- Logo: `header.makeStylesroot-*.themed-logo-background-primary-fill > a.MuiLink-root > img.makeStyleslogoContainer-*`.
- `nav.makeStylesnavigation-*` > `ul.makeStylesroot-*` > items `a.MuiButtonBase-root.MuiListItemButton-root` with hrefs
  `/ultra/profile`, `/ultra/institution-page`, `/ultra/stream`, `/ultra/course`, `/ultra/organization`, `/ultra/calendar`,
  `/ultra/messages`, `/ultra/grades`, `/ultra/tools`, and `#` (sign out).
- Footer: three `a.MuiLink-underlineHover` (Privacy, Terms, Accessibility; locale paths).
- **`makeStyles*-N-N-N` suffixes are generated.** Match on MUI's stable class names, `[class*="makeStylesnavigation"]`,
  and the hrefs. `theme.css`'s `nav#base_tools` / `:has(#base_tools)` rules don't match this school's nav.
