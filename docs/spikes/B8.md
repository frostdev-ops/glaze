# B8 — Ultra course page structure for course tabs (evidence)

The orchestrator made one read-only pass on 2026-10-15, in a Chrome **without Glaze**, signed in to a real Ultra school.
It opened one course's outline from the student's own course list, and opened no thread, test or attempt. It recorded
structure only.

- Route: `/ultra/courses/_N_N/outline`. The course opens in `div.course-content-container` >
  `div.panel-wrap.hide-in-background.modern` (`position: absolute`), a panel over the base layout. Items opened from
  it (documents, discussions, assignments) open as further panels on top.
- **The course's top bar:** `div.bb-course-navigation` (**`position: sticky`, 94px, z-index 10**) > `bb-course-navigation`.
  It has two rows of about 47px:
  - Row 1, the header: home, "Courses ▾" (the course switcher), the course's name or code, and its status (OPEN).
  - Row 2, the course's tools: `div.makeStylestoolbar-*` > `div.js-course-navigation` > `div.makeStylesscrollArea-*` >
    `nav` > `ul.bb-ui-navigation-list-horizontal` > `li` > `a.MuiListItemButton-root.js-course-<tool>-tool` (46px).
- **Tools** (stable `js-course-*-tool` classes; hrefs):
  - `outline` `/outline`
  - `calendar` `/calendar`
  - `announcement` `/announcements`
  - `discussion` `/engagement`
  - `grades` `/grades`
  - `messages` `/messages`
  - `groupsStudent` `/groups/enrollments`
  - `achievements` `/achievements`
  - Details links on the outline: `/outline/roster`, `/outline/attendanceGrade?courseId=…`,
    `/outline/booksAndTools?parentId=…`.
- The `makeStyles*-N-N-N` suffixes are generated. Match on `js-course-*` classes, `bb-course-navigation`, and hrefs.
