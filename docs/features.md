# Features

Everything Glaze does, site by site. What changed in each version is in the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md); what is kept and sent where is in the [privacy summary](privacy.md).

Glaze is a Chrome and Safari extension that restyles Blackboard Ultra, Canvas and Moodle. It replaces the palette with one you
control, reshapes the pages around it, and adds small helpers you can see at a glance. It works on any
institution's Blackboard Ultra install, because every Ultra deployment lives under `/ultra/` on its own
domain, and on Canvas and Moodle, which Glaze recognizes on any school's domain.

Ultra's in-page route changes remain visible while Blackboard builds them. Only the initial document paint can wait
for Glaze's local stylesheets, with the existing one-second limit.
If Glaze is reloaded while an LMS tab stays open, its old extension API work stops until that tab reloads too.
Its animated scene also stops and releases its rendering resources.

The settings editor has eight tabs: **Look, Blackboard, Canvas, Moodle, Study tools, Sharing and sync, Backup and privacy, and About**. Search works across tabs; section links such as `#blackboard/nav` and older editor links reveal the right tab. Section resets change their own preferences; resetting preferences keeps your palettes, pictures, local notes/tasks, bookmarks and share keys. The live preview uses fictional local pages at real desktop or phone CSS widths, with site/page/appearance controls. Focusing a Blackboard course's editor controls previews that course's appearance; Blackboard nicknames can be edited here as well as on its course cards. Sharing and sync includes the Relay origin and an explicit connection test; saving stays offline.

## Mods

From Glaze 3.0.4: add backgrounds, animations, widgets, nav layouts and page tweaks made by others, each showing what it reads and why before you add it, or build your own widget with no code. Reviewed mods are in the [Mods gallery](https://frostdev.io/glaze/mods). Looks can have video and animated backgrounds too.

**User-installed mods:** Chrome can run JavaScript from mod bundles you choose to install, including author-signed channel updates. Glaze does not fetch mod code to run automatically from us. Code runs in an isolated sandbox without extension APIs; Firefox and Safari use declarative parts only. Courses, to-dos and grades are available only with declared and granted permissions, and only to a DOM-less Worker. Brokered network requests require a declared and granted exact HTTPS host and omit cookies and referrers. A mod with both data and network access can send that data to its granted hosts. A mod without data access may use a frame UI that can contact other sites; linked Look media also contacts its chosen host. Mod bundles, grants, settings and per-mod storage stay on this device. See [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md) for channels and the optional signed blocklist.

## Surfaces

**Look → Surfaces** controls content-panel opacity, blur, palette/custom color, accent/course tint, shadow strength/color, frost, depth and borders. Panels, native content rows, navigation, menus, tooltips and Glaze’s own apps share those controls across Blackboard Ultra, Canvas and Moodle. Surface backgrounds follow the Look’s corner radius. Automatic defaults retain each Look’s existing fills; custom transparency needs blur and a non-solid background. Small rows get no additional backdrop blur.

## Pets

All ten bundled companions, including Classic buddy, Cat and Bunny, have new climbing, sleeping, playing, peeking, XP and level-up reaction animations. Imported v1/v2 characters retain compatible fallback poses.

Choose one pet character for the habitat, Study buddy, Blackboard navigation badge and course-card pet. The original Cat and Bunny join the character selector, and Blackboard file previews refresh expired viewer tickets and retry while uploaded files convert.

**Animated pets:** the optional habitat keeps its existing XP and decorations. Choose Ember the fox, Luna the owl, Mochi the cat, Sprout the dragon, Pebble the capybara, Rime the arctic fox or the animated Glaze droplet, or import a local ChatGPT pet under **Study tools → Pet habitat**. Choose its `pet.json` and PNG/WebP sprite sheet together, a ZIP containing them, or the sprite sheet alone. Existing v1 (`1536 × 1872`) and v2 (`1536 × 2288`) sheets are supported; v2 adds sixteen gaze directions. Click a pet to wave, or focus it and press Enter/Space to jump. Animation pauses offscreen and in hidden pages, and reduced motion or Glaze Motion off shows a still frame. No account connection, pet service or upload is needed.

Your character choice is shared across this device: the habitat, Study buddy widget, navigation badge and course-card pet all use it. Each display keeps its own visibility setting. The original Cat and Bunny are also available in the habitat's character selector; their original artwork has idle and action poses. All displays wake during a focus timer, celebrate a completed focus block or a task checked in Better To Do, and respond to petting where interactive. These reactions add no XP. Progress, outfits and habitat decorations stay with each school's signed-in account. Removing a selected imported pet switches back to Classic buddy without removing progress or decorations. The navigation level uses the signed-in account directly and does not depend on planner-cache freshness.

## Share sessions

**Optional share sessions:** everything lives in **Settings → Sharing and sync → Share sessions** (Ctrl/⌘K and To Do open it too). In Notes, **Share** on a note shares it in one step and copies an edit invite; **Shared notes** lists the account's shared notes with their invites and joins a pasted invite straight into Notes. Nothing connects until you create, join or resume. Share selected notes/a folder (including encrypted pictures and attachments), your current Look, or selected custom tasks with their steps and plans. Paste invites into Glaze rather than opening them in a web page. Anyone holding an edit invite can edit; a view invite can only read. Joining keeps a separate local copy; explicitly copy into a chosen local account to add new items. An editable copy follows updates; a viewer's copy is detached. A Look changes your theme only after **Use this Look** opens the existing editor preview and you apply it.

While a shared surface is open, changes sync through `https://share.frostdev.io` by default, end-to-end encrypted, with catch-up on reopening. **Pause sharing** stops connections; Glaze and your local copies still work without the relay. **Leave this device** forgets its keys; only the creator can delete the session from the relay. App copies remain. Sessions stay until deleted unless the creator opts into deletion after a year unused. The device list shows self-chosen editing-device names and last-seen times, not verified identities; view-only devices cannot announce themselves. Pause, Leave and Delete remain available while a sync is busy; old queued updates cannot reconnect afterward.

**Share sessions → Relay** can select a custom HTTPS origin, or HTTP `localhost`/`127.0.0.1` for local testing. Saving only changes the synced preference for **new** sessions; existing sessions and backup keys keep their own server. **Test connection** explicitly checks `/health` with a five-second timeout and never enables sharing. Joining a custom-server invite first names the host and asks for confirmation in Glaze, before permission checks or connections. Restoring custom keys stays offline until Resume confirms any new hosts. Custom operators receive encrypted data and metadata under their own policies; Glaze cannot vouch for them. See [Run your own relay](../server/README.md) for Node/Docker, TLS proxies, disk/R2 storage, limits, backups and updates.

Notes **Back up or restore** opens **Settings → Backup and privacy → Notes backup** for that account. Both notes ZIPs and settings backups always include all share keys and creator delete tokens, even while sharing is paused: anyone with the backup can open those sessions and may change or delete them. Keep backups private. Restoring keys does not connect; resume explicitly. After a relay restore, **Re-upload retained files** sends this device's retained bytes encrypted again and publishes fresh references. Missing local bytes cannot be recreated. Caps are 20 sessions, 200 selected notes/tasks, 16 MiB session state, 64 MiB files per session and 200 MiB retained session files per device. Additional carriers need an optional adapter; arbitrary storage and LMS assignments are not shared.

## Everything else

- **Course cards as widgets on Canvas** — the first time the Dashboard's board has nothing on it, the cards of Canvas's card view become its widgets, in Canvas's order (a board you empty yourself stays empty); **Edit layout → + Course cards** adds or removes courses. Canvas's own card view steps aside while they are there; **Show Canvas’s course cards** brings it back (for moving or hiding cards in Canvas), and **Show course card widgets** returns. They read only what Canvas's Dashboard already asked for.
- **Course cards as widgets on Ultra** — opt in with **Edit layout → + Course cards** on Courses, or pick **+ Course card** on the Dashboard. Each card moves, snaps, resizes, folds and removes like other tiles; each board keeps its own layout, up to 48 course cards per school. The cards start in the native list’s area, in course/term order. **Show Ultra’s course list** restores its search, filters and term picker; **Show course cards** returns to your tiles. Removing every course card or hiding the board restores the native list. Cards reuse loaded metadata, grades, progress, new-work badges, bookmarks and stored tasks; unavailable data stays absent until the existing readers answer. No extra course, gradebook or planner request is made.
- **Blackboard Ultra appearance** — the Courses page shares Canvas’s card editor and appearance settings: colors, gradients, pictures, crop, filters and overlay; grid sizing, condensed cards, hover effects, particles and pets. Open a course card's Glaze menu, then **Card style and bookmarks**. In Ultra list view, banner styles and corners apply; row dimensions and labels stay native. Ultra's document viewer stays native pending a verified viewer origin and safe integration.
- **Blackboard global widgets** — a widget you choose to float stays available across Blackboard pages, including the native Institution tab, with one copy. It keeps clear of either navigation rail, course tab strips and the footer; a small window gets a scrolling stack. The Dashboard's own layout stays separate.

- **Palettes** — six presets, 14 colour tokens, your own saved palettes, and automatic light/dark palettes that follow the system or a schedule (dark from 20:00 to 08:00, say). Keep quizzes light (off by default) leaves quizzes you're taking and external tools such as New Quizzes unthemed on Canvas.
- **Look** — fonts for text, headings and code (two bundled; about forty more from Google Fonts and Nerd Fonts, or any Google Font by name, each previewed in its own face), text size, line height, corner radius, density, depth and glass.
- **Backgrounds** — solid; a gradient you shape (linear, radial, conic or mesh; angle, blend, spread); a drifting aurora; your own image; a looping video, animated picture (WebP or AVIF) or Lottie animation from a link or a file (always muted, held on its first frame when motion is off, paused while the tab is hidden); or ten slow animated scenes (three.js, drawn at low resolution; two for Halloween, a moonlit graveyard and a pumpkin patch) with one Intensity slider. Palette colors or your own.

  addresses so the browser can recover from a cached failure. Changing the background or turning
  Glaze off and on permits another attempt; refused shaders and missing media keep their fallback.

- **Course colors** — every course its own color, from Ultra's stripes blended into your palette or set one by one, on course pages, Messages, Grades and the calendar.
- **Tooltips and hints** — themed tooltips. Hover a date for how far off it is, a grade for its share.
- **Extras** — score bars, due-date status ("in 2 days · Unopened", "Submitted", "Submitted late", "Graded · 18/20") and grade percentages, folder previews, course progress, new-grade and new-content badges, a what-if grade calculator that keeps your scores, a Ctrl+K command palette for courses, items, pages, settings and what's due this week (also in the popup) that also searches your courses on Canvas and Ultra, remembering your place in a course's content, a backup of what you type in Blackboard's editors, Download all, which saves a folder's files as one zip, and focus mode for course documents (on by default); due-date urgency, shorter descriptions, reading width, keyboard shortcuts, dark documents (dimmed or inverted) and hiding page parts you don't use (off by default).

## Glaze navigation

On by default on Blackboard Ultra, Canvas and Moodle, Glaze draws its own navigation in place of the site's, set
up in Settings → **Navigation**. **Placement**: a side rail, open with labels or icons only (with a tooltip on each and
a peek when you point at it or move focus into it), or a top bar whose items move under **More** when they don't fit.
**Style**: floating glass with a little room around it, or embedded at the page's edge. **Inside a course** it can stay
the same, become icons only, become a top bar, or hide (the course's own Home button remains). **Course tabs** show in it
too: click one to switch, the middle button or Delete closes it, and P pins it. Course tab settings (Settings →
Navigation → Course tabs) can keep them **only in the navigation**, with no strip, in course pages and their frames alike.
The logo can be Glaze's, the school's, **your own picture** (kept on this device; a shared Look shows Glaze's logo in its
place) or none. On a phone it is
a bottom bar of four tabs and More, whose sheet holds the rest, the course tabs, your account, signing out and
**Customize navigation**. The page keeps the bar's room from first paint, and the chevron and pin are saved for every
tab.

On Moodle, the bar replaces the top navigation and phone hamburger. Profile, notifications and conversations open in Glaze panels; messages link to Moodle's full messaging page. A course's secondary navigation, including More links, appears in the rail or the top bar's second row when **Course menu** is on. The course index and block drawers remain available. Turning navigation off restores Moodle's own navigation.

**Navigation items** renames, re-icons, reorders and groups every item, hides any of them, picks the four phone tabs,
adds up to eight links of your own, and turns on Notes, To Do, search and Glaze settings (off by default). Its
**Widgets** put the clock, what's due next, the focus timer and your pet at the top or bottom of the rail (a square each
in the icons rail, with the details in its tip), compact in a top bar and in a phone's More sheet; they are the same
timer and pet as your other widgets, and nothing more is recorded. Mods' navigation widgets sit under the rail's items,
and with a top bar or on a phone they open over the page from a **Widgets** button, without loading again. A worn Look's
navigation layout and your older navigation links, hidden items, order and widgets still apply under these choices. Look codes
carry how the bar looks and sits (never whether it is on); Export all carries your items. On Canvas, Courses, Groups,
Account, History, Help and Admin open as Glaze panels with Canvas's own session data. Course links and term names,
account settings, recent pages and the school's help links stay within their permitted account. The course's own
menu joins the rail or a second row below a top bar; switch that option off to keep Canvas's course menu. Other
items open through the site's own links, and the site's own navigation stays in the page, out of sight,
coming back when the switch is off. Where Blackboard opens a course at its own address without its drawer, the items
last seen on this device stand in. Motion follows Glaze's motion setting and your system's reduced motion;
right-to-left pages mirror it.
