                            Grades and GPA

                            Grades and GPA

                            Canvas · Blackboard
                            Beside each due date in course content, where the work stands: "in 2 days · Unopened", "Submitted", "Graded · 18/20"; and the percentage in every grade.
                            Canvas · Blackboard · Moodle
                            Each course card shows your overall grade, in its score band's color, when your instructor shares it. On Canvas: click the grade to open the course's grades.
                            "New" beside grades that arrived since you last opened a course's gradebook, and a count on your course cards. On Canvas: the count follows Canvas's own unread grades.
                            On a course's gradebook: try scores for work not graded yet, or set a target and see what you'd need, by the course's own rules. Kept on this device until the real grade arrives; where the rules aren't known, it says it estimates.
                            Your GPA from your current grades: on Canvas's Dashboard for each term, on Blackboard's Grades for this term. Set each course's credits, level and grading scale there.

                            Syllabus and insights

                            Canvas · Blackboard
                            Read a course's syllabus on this device, from its page, a file or pasted text. You check every field; then the professor, class times and dates show on the course, and can go to your GPA and To Do.

                            Course content

                            Course content

                            Canvas · Blackboard
                            "New" beside course items added since you last looked at a course, with a count on folders holding some and on your course cards.
                            A bar at the top of course content adding up how much of the course you have completed. On Canvas: from the modules' requirements; a course without any shows no bar.
                            Hover a closed folder in course content to see what is inside, what is done and what is due.
                            A button on each folder, learning module or Canvas module that saves its files as one zip. Items you haven't opened are left out, so none is marked viewed.

                            Search and shortcuts

                            Search and shortcuts

                            Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) to jump to a course, an item in this course, a page or a Glaze setting.
                            Canvas · Blackboard
                            Search your courses from the command palette: titles on Canvas, and on Blackboard released document, announcement and discussion text too.
