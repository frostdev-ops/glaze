                              Typing

                              Typing

                              Blackboard
                              Fix typos
                              Common slips that are no word, such as “teh” or “recieve”, become the word you meant as you finish typing them. Undo puts yours back.
                              Capital I and sentence starts
                              A lone “i” becomes “I”, and a sentence’s first word gets its capital once you type past it. Names elsewhere are left as you wrote them.
                              Doubled words and spaces
                              A word typed twice in a row, such as “the the”, loses the second, and two spaces become one. Doubles like “had had” are kept.
                              Smart quotes and dashes
                              Straight quotes and apostrophes curl as you type, and two hyphens become a dash. Off, every character goes in exactly as typed.
                              Ask how to paste formatting
                              After you paste text with its own formatting, a choice to keep that formatting or match the paper’s. Off, pasted text keeps its formatting.
