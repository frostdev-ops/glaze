        Background

        Behind the page
        Scene

        How much it stands out: color, contrast and pace together. Drawn with WebGL (three.js) at low resolution, at most 30 frames a second; it pauses in background tabs and holds still when motion is off.

        Style

        Blend: how softly the colors melt into each other (0 gives crisp bands). Spread: how much of the screen the change covers; past it, the end colors hold.

        Motion

        Level

        Subtle: pages and cards ease in, panels slide, hover lifts. Expressive adds springier motion, a drifting aurora, livelier animated backgrounds and a small burst when you complete an item. Off also holds animated backgrounds still.
