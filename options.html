                            Connection

                            Your sessions

                            Create a session

                            Only the selected notes, Look or custom tasks and their plans are shared. Course account identifiers and LMS assignments are not carried.

                            Join from an invite link

                            Paste the full link here. Anyone holding an edit link can change the session; anyone holding a view link can read it. Keep links private.

                            Joining keeps a separate local copy. Copy it into Notes or To Do to add new local items without replacing existing work.

                            Relay

                            Relay

                            Choose the server for newly created share sessions.

                            On this device

                            The relay applies to new sessions only. Existing sessions keep their own server. Saving never connects.

                            Use an HTTPS origin without a path, query, fragment or credentials. HTTP is allowed only on localhost or 127.0.0.1 for self-testing.

                            With a custom relay, encrypted data goes to its operator. Glaze cannot vouch for that server.

                            Google Calendar sync

                            Google Calendar sync

                            Connect a calendar only when you choose to sign in.

                            Google Chrome

                            Google Calendar sync

                            Keeps a calendar named Glaze in your Google account up to date with Canvas or Blackboard: the events Export calendar (.ics) would save, with the choices above. Off until you connect. Chrome signs you in with Google; Glaze asks only to manage calendars it makes, and touches nothing else in your Google Calendar. There's no Glaze server: your calendar goes only between this browser and Google.

                            Google Calendar sync works in Google Chrome. In this browser, save your calendar with Export calendar (.ics) and import the file into your calendar.

                            Syncing on another computer already? Paste its link code instead, so both keep the same Glaze calendar.

                            On your other computer, open Glaze's settings and paste this code under Google Calendar sync. It's also the calendar's ID in Google Calendar's settings.
