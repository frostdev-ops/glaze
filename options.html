        Browse fonts

        Web fonts download when you choose them: from Google Fonts, or jsDelivr for OpenDyslexic and the Nerd Fonts (about 5 MB each). Opening this list loads small previews from Google Fonts.

        Background

        Behind the page
        Scene

        How much it stands out: color, contrast and pace together. Drawn with WebGL (three.js) at low resolution, at most 30 frames a second; it pauses in background tabs and holds still when motion is off.

        Colors
        Style

        For Radial, the angle is where the centre sits.

        Blend: how softly the colors melt into each other (0 gives crisp bands). Spread: how much of the screen the change covers; past it, the end colors hold.

        An https link to a picture. If it can't load, the gradient shows instead.

        Aurora drifts slowly when motion is set to Expressive.

        Just the palette's ground color.

        Motion

        Level

        Subtle: pages and cards ease in, panels slide, hover lifts. Expressive adds springier motion, a drifting aurora, livelier animated backgrounds and a small burst when you complete an item. Off also holds animated backgrounds still.

        Courses

        Courses

          Organizations

            Open Blackboard or Canvas once and your courses show up here. Change Canvas colours in its own card menu.

            Course cards

            Canvas's Dashboard cards. Give a card its own color, gradient or picture, and bookmarks, from its course menu on the Dashboard.

            Hover
            Particles
            Pet

            The pet sits on your first card. Hover effects, particles and the pet move only while motion is on (Motion, above); with it off the pet sits still.

            Extras

            Extras work on Blackboard and Canvas. On Canvas they sit where Canvas keeps the same things: Modules for course content, the Assignments list and To Do for due dates, the Dashboard for course cards.

            On Moodle: what's due this week, the command palette and grades on course cards (Dashboard and My courses).

            Better To Do

            Notes and widgets

            Reminders and streak

            Each is off until you turn it on. Reminders show only on Canvas pages, in the tab you're looking at: a small note in the corner, never a browser notification. They stay on this device and change nothing in Canvas. The bell in the To Do header and Ctrl+K "Reminders" list what's coming up.

            Share and back up

            Your palette, fonts, look and background as one line of text, to send to someone with Glaze.

            Gallery

            Free Looks shared by other students. Pick one there and Glaze opens it here to preview before anything changes. Opening the gallery is an ordinary visit to frostdev.io; Glaze sends nothing.

            Open the gallery

            Favorites

            Themes you saved from the gallery or a theme code, kept on this computer.

              History

              The themes you wore lately, newest first, kept on this computer.

                Exports are a JSON file with your palette, presets, options, any course nicknames you set, and your Better To Do tasks and plans. Course names from Blackboard or Canvas stay on this computer and are not exported.

                Card styles and bookmarks go in the export too. Pictures kept for cards stay on this computer.

                Report a problem

                Opens a new issue on Glaze's GitHub page with your Glaze version, browser, system and language filled in, and nothing about your school. GitHub receives that text when the page opens; nothing is posted until you add what happened and submit it.
