Turn on Glaze in Safari

Glaze is installed. Two steps in Safari and you’re done.

  1. 1 Tick Glaze in Settings

    In Safari’s Settings → Extensions, select the checkbox beside Glaze for Blackboard Ultra.

    Waiting for Safari…✓ Glaze is on

    Extensions
    Glaze for Blackboard Ultra

  2. 2 Allow it on Blackboard

    Open your Blackboard Ultra site, click the Glaze button in Safari’s toolbar and choose Always Allow on This Website. Then reload the page.

    Allow for One Day Always Allow on This Website

That’s it. You can close this window; Safari keeps Glaze on.
