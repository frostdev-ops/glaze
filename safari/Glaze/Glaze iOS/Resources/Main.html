Turn on Glaze in Safari

Your colors on Blackboard Ultra, Canvas and Moodle.

  1. 1 Turn Glaze on in Settings

    Open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions, tap Glaze and turn on Allow Extension.

    Checking Safari…

    Glaze
    Allow Extension

  2. 2 Allow it on your school’s website

    In Safari, open Blackboard Ultra, Canvas or Moodle and tap the extensions button in the address bar: the puzzle piece, or on iPhone the page menu at the left. Tap Glaze, then Always Allow… and Always Allow on This Website. Then reload the page.

    Glaze!
    Always Allow on This Website
    school.edu🧩

Glaze is enabled. Allow it on your school’s website too, then open Glaze from Safari’s extensions menu.

Next steps and permissions

Choose Open the editor in Glaze’s Safari popup to try Looks and settings with the built-in sample page.

Glaze’s enabled state and access to each website are separate. Check the profile you use for school and, if needed, the private browsing switch in Safari’s extension settings.

You can close this app. Safari manages the extension’s background activity; Background App Refresh for this setup guide is not required.

Updates

The App Store updates Glaze and its Safari extension. Manage automatic app updates in Settings → Apps → App Store.
