Turn on Glaze in Safari

Two steps and Blackboard Ultra wears your colors.

  1. 1 Turn Glaze on in Settings

    Open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions, tap Glaze for Blackboard Ultra and turn on Allow Extension.

    Glaze is off✓ Glaze is on

    Glaze for Blackboard Ultra
    Allow Extension

  2. 2 Allow it on Blackboard

    In Safari, open your Blackboard Ultra site and tap the extensions button in the address bar: the puzzle piece, or on iPhone the page menu at the left. Tap Glaze for Blackboard Ultra, then Always Allow… and Always Allow on This Website. Then reload the page.

    Glaze for Blackboard Ultra!
    Always Allow on This Website
    school.edu🧩

That’s it. Your colors and settings are under Glaze, from the same button in Safari.
