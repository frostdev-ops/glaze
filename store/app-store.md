## Description (4,000 character maximum)

Make Blackboard Ultra, Canvas and Moodle feel like your own study space. Glaze is a Safari extension that brings your colors, fonts and backgrounds to your school's website, with planning and study tools beside your coursework.

YOUR LOOK
Pick a Look or set colors, fonts and surfaces. Add a gradient, picture or animated background, pair light and dark palettes, and share a theme code. Motion follows your reduced-motion setting.

YOUR WORKSPACE
On Blackboard Ultra and Canvas, arrange course cards and widgets on a Glaze Dashboard. Keep Better To Do in the sidebar, open course tabs, customize navigation and find courses, pages and settings with the command palette.

PLAN AND STUDY
Better To Do combines coursework, tasks, steps and plans. Keep notes with folders, pictures and attachments, use a focus timer and pick an animated companion. Review imported schedules or syllabi on your device, then export plans as a calendar file.

Due-date status, course progress, badges for new content and grades, and what-if scores depend on your platform and school. Real grades never change.

Announcements open in a Glaze pop-up, with hover previews. Assignment rubrics show your published scores and feedback, in the page on Blackboard Ultra when there's room, otherwise in the pop-up.

Moodle Boost gets the same Looks, course totals when available, a due list from Timeline and keyboard shortcuts.

GLAZE WRITER AND DOCUMENTS
On Blackboard Ultra, Glaze Writer replaces the editor in discussions, journals, messages, assignments and tests. Spelling and grammar run on your device; tests get proofing only. Full writer has pages in MLA, APA, Chicago or your own format, with citations, footnotes, versions and .docx files. Documents keeps papers, spreadsheets with Excel formulas and existing charts, fillable PDFs and PowerPoint decks to view and present. Deleted files stay in Trash for 30 days. Start an assignment as Document or Spreadsheet. Submit uploaded PDFs and decks in their format, and spreadsheets as .xlsx. AI is optional and off by default. Tab completion only finishes the current sentence; Feedback follows the rubric and instructions. Glaze never drafts.

PRIVACY AND CONTROL
Glaze is free, with no ads, analytics or Glaze account. Glaze settings, notes, tasks, coursework records, papers and documents stay on your device. Optional share sessions start only when you create, join or resume one, and encrypt content before it goes through your chosen relay. Fonts, media, external players and AI providers you choose, and citation lookups you click, can make network requests. Glaze does not collect or sell your data.

Glaze never changes grades, and rubrics are read-only. Writer sends what you write only through Blackboard's own buttons, when you press them. Optional Canvas planner ticks, course colors and nicknames are saved to your school account. Opening marks unread Canvas announcements read; Moodle marks posts read when forum tracking is automatic. Safari supports declarative mod content and does not run downloaded mod JavaScript.

GET STARTED
Open Glaze for the setup guide, turn on the extension in Safari and allow it on your school's website. It works on school domains and on hosted Blackboard Ultra, Canvas and Moodle sites.
