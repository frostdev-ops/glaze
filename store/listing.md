# Chrome Web Store listing — Glaze 4.0.0

## Name

Glaze: Themes for Canvas, Blackboard & Moodle

## Short summary (132 character maximum)

Canvas, Blackboard & Moodle themes and dark mode. Planner, GPA and notes on Canvas & Blackboard.

## Category

Productivity

## Detailed description

Give Canvas, Blackboard Ultra and Moodle your own theme and dark mode. On Canvas and Blackboard Ultra, Glaze adds a student planner, to-do list, GPA calculator and notes. Glaze Writer replaces Blackboard Ultra's editor, and Documents keeps your papers, spreadsheets, PDFs and slides in one place. Free, with no Glaze account, ads or analytics.

YOUR LOOK

• Apply one of eleven Looks, or get free ones at frostdev.io/glaze/looks; Looks you save or use from the gallery appear wherever you pick one, even for a single course.
• Dark mode: pair a light and a dark palette with your system's appearance or a schedule.
• Custom colors and fonts: edit all fourteen colors, give each course its own color, and pick Lexend, OpenDyslexic or any Google Font.
• Backgrounds: a gradient, your own picture or one of eleven animated scenes.
• Pointers in Look › Pointer, and typing effects that never read what you type and stay out of password fields and quizzes.
• Glaze navigation, and Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) to jump to any course, page or setting.

PLAN AND STUDY ON CANVAS AND BLACKBOARD ULTRA

• Better To Do: coursework grouped by due date, with your own tasks and focus sessions.
• Announcements open in a Glaze pop-up from Better To Do or Ctrl+K on Canvas and Blackboard Ultra, and from the sites' own lists, including Moodle. Hover to preview in Better To Do and those lists.
• Due-date reminders in the page, never browser notifications, off until you turn them on.
• Import your class schedule or a syllabus from a PDF or photo, read on your device, and export deadlines as a calendar file (.ics).
• Notes with folders and attachments, a focus timer and a pet habitat.
• Course tabs keep up to 4 course pages live alongside the main page.

GLAZE WRITER AND DOCUMENTS

• On Blackboard Ultra, Glaze Writer takes the place of the editor in discussions, journals, messages, assignments and tests; ⋯ › Blackboard editor gives a box back to Blackboard.
• Assignments open in a light editor whose text goes into Blackboard's text box, so Blackboard's own Submit sends it. Full writer gives real pages in MLA 9, APA 7, Chicago 17, Blank or your own format, with format checks, footnotes, versions, .docx import and export, and a submit sheet that attaches the .docx.
• Spelling, grammar and autocorrect run on your device. Cite looks up a DOI, ISBN or web address only when you click; sources stay with each assignment, journal, discussion or course's new messages.
• AI is optional and off until you set it up in Settings › AI. Tab completion only finishes the current sentence, and Feedback assesses your paper against the rubric and instructions. Glaze never drafts. Tests get proofing only.
• Documents keeps every paper, spreadsheet, PDF and PowerPoint deck in one place, from Glaze's toolbar icon, Ctrl+K or Glaze navigation. Start a paper in Blank, MLA, APA or Chicago, or a spreadsheet, or import a file; deleted files wait 30 days in Trash.
• Spreadsheets have more than 380 Excel functions and draw an .xlsx's own charts on the grid; they import .xlsx, .csv and .tsv files and export .xlsx that Excel opens without repair. PDFs open with zoom, find, fillable forms you can save, and export; decks open with a slide rail, zoom, find, speaker notes and Present. PDFs and decks up to 50 MB stay in this browser.
• In an assignment, an empty attempt offers Document or Spreadsheet, and an uploaded PDF, deck or spreadsheet opens in its viewer and is submitted as itself. Settings › Writer turns each kind off.
• Documents wears your Look, and Writer's pages follow a dark Look; Light page keeps them white.

AGENTS

• Agents is off by default in Settings › AI › Agents. Connect AI apps on your computer through the glaze-mcp helper. Approve each app and each data group separately: courses, to-dos and schedule; grades; assignments and rubrics; Writer documents. Every change, including a to-do tick, Writer fix or citation, waits for your OK. Agents cannot submit work.

CANVAS

• A GPA calculator, plus what-if grades that follow Canvas's grading rules.
• Folded assignment rubrics open in a Glaze pop-up with your published scores and feedback.
• Download all for a module's files. Items you haven't opened are left out, and redirected files stop with an error asking you to download them from Canvas.
• Glaze themes Canvas's own what-if scores, editor recovery, nicknames, colors, Files-folder zips and unread counts; it does not replace them.

BLACKBOARD ULTRA

• Grades: score bars, what-if grades and a GPA calculator with past terms and a goal.
• Assignment rubrics, with your published scores and feedback, show in the page when there's room, otherwise in the pop-up.
• Course Content: due-date status, course progress and new-content badges.
• Download all zips files from items you've already opened, so unopened items aren't marked viewed.

MOODLE

• Glaze styles Moodle's Boost theme (Moodle 5, and 4.x where the markup matches), with course totals on cards, what's due this week and Glaze navigation.

AT YOUR SCHOOL

Works from install on instructure.com, blackboard.com and moodlecloud.com. Install access also covers frostdev.io and share.frostdev.io; other hosts are requested when needed. Add your school's own address in Settings › Privacy and data › Sites; Back up… and Restore… there save everything in one file. Glaze never changes grades. What you write reaches Blackboard only through its own Post, Reply, Send or Submit, when you press it; Full writer's submit sheet attaches your .docx and submits it after you confirm. What-if scores stay on your device.

PRIVACY

Settings follow Chrome or Firefox sync if it is on, including course nicknames, GPA choices and some school, course and account identifiers. Coursework records, notes, tasks, typing backups and Writer's papers, documents, files you open and sources are stored on your device. AI is off by default. If you enable it for a feature, an on-device model works locally; a provider or endpoint you choose receives that feature's context, such as your draft and assignment materials. With AI Feedback on, assignment pictures can be read before Review; up to 128 checked readings stay locally for 30 days. AI outline sends sections while Outline is open; AI source extraction sends bibliography entries while Sources is open. Both have their own switches. Cite sends a DOI to doi.org, Crossref or DataCite, an ISBN to Open Library, or a web address to its own site, only when you click Look up. Agents is off by default. Turning it on in Settings › AI › Agents asks for optional permission to connect only to the glaze-mcp helper on your computer. Each app receives only the data groups you allow for it. Glaze sends nothing to FrostDev. An app you allow may send what it gets to its own AI service under its own policy. Glaze reads your LMS with your session and writes only when you ask (a planner tick, a Canvas nickname or color, Sign out, Moodle's Edit mode, Writer's Post, Reply, Send or Submit, marking announcements read when opened in Canvas or in Moodle forums with automatic read tracking, or a change an agent app asks for and you allow in Glaze's window: marking a Canvas planner item done); previews and Blackboard send no read mark. Rubrics are read-only. Chosen web fonts and background images make ordinary network requests. Linked videos and animations are fetched without cookies, referrers or redirects; identifying container metadata is removed, but encoded streams and visible pixels remain. Older files that cannot be cleaned stay local and are not shared; course tab sketches stay in memory and are never sent anywhere. Sharing starts only when you create, join or resume a session. Optional share sessions and mod channels are encrypted so the relay cannot read them; a mod granted course data and network access can send that data to its granted hosts. These services receive connection data such as your IP address. Glaze does not collect or sell your data. Full policy: https://github.com/frostdev-ops/glaze/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

## Single-purpose statement

Glaze customizes how Blackboard Ultra, Canvas and Moodle pages look and read, and helps users plan, write and keep their own coursework on those pages. Its themes and optional helpers present the user's existing course information, keep the user's own tasks, notes, drafts and documents locally, save accessible files the user chooses and, with Agents on, give AI apps the user approves on their own computer the data they allow. Every permission serves that purpose.

## Permission justifications

### storage

Saves theme and extra settings in chrome.storage.sync, including user-set course nicknames, colors, tint and Looks; these follow Chrome sync when enabled. Local storage keeps course details, seen-grade and content records, the due list, Better To Do's own tasks and plans, the class schedule, reminder and streak state, widget layouts, hidden folders, supported draft backups, Writer's papers, documents, versions, dictionary and saved sources, what-if scores and course positions, and the sites the user approved. Course tab sketches (boxes, colours and the course's name, with its page path) stay in page memory and chrome.storage.session (memory). Canvas badge records include site, account and course identifiers; some Ultra seen-grade/content records still use course ids without account scoping. Drafts expire after at most 14 days; switching backup off deletes them. Coursework records, papers and draft text are not synced. The privacy policy gives each record's retention and removal.

### unlimitedStorage

Notes, their pictures and attachments, Writer's papers and documents (PDFs and decks up to 50 MB each), course-card pictures and files kept for Look backgrounds are kept in the extension's own storage. This permission stops the browser from deleting them to free space. Nothing more is collected or sent because of it.

### Host access (1,000 character maximum)

Required hosts: *.instructure.com, *.blackboard.com, *.moodlecloud.com, frostdev.io and share.frostdev.io. https://*/* is optional: the popup checks the page before offering Use Glaze on the site; the first-run and Settings address fields check LMS APIs after the user grants access, returning only new grants if no LMS answers. Browser-menu grants are checked too, and unused if checks fail. All sites grants every HTTPS site; Glaze then asks the user first on each new school's pages. Redirected files stop with a Canvas fallback. Other hosts are requested as needed: Canvas Download all file servers, Gradescope, Look media, Cite lookups, AI providers, mod network hosts and custom share relays. Local testing uses optional http://localhost/* and http://127.0.0.1/*. On allowed school sites Glaze reads with the user's session and writes only on their click (planner ticks, Canvas nicknames and colors, announcement read marks, Sign out, Moodle's Edit mode, Writer's Post, Reply, Send, Submit).

### activeTab

When the user opens Glaze's toolbar popup, Glaze reads that tab's address and checks its markup for Canvas, Blackboard Ultra or Moodle with a packaged function, so the popup can offer to turn Glaze on for that site. It reads nothing beyond that check and keeps nothing unless the user allows the site.

### declarativeContent

Puts a dot on Glaze's toolbar icon on pages that look like Canvas, Blackboard Ultra or Moodle, with page-URL and CSS rules that Chrome evaluates itself, so the user can see where Glaze can be turned on. Glaze is not told which pages match.

### scripting

Registers Glaze's packaged content scripts on the sites the user allowed, so later visits are themed from the start, and runs the popup's LMS check in the current tab. With All sites granted, it also registers a packaged check that reads only a page's head. All code is packaged; nothing is fetched and run. It does not inject Glaze's theme into unrelated sites.

### Web-accessible resources

The theme stylesheets (theme.css, motion.css, extras.css, canvas.css, moodle.css, features/apps.css, features/lookx.css), bundled fonts, logos, stickers, pointers and pet sprites provide the theme in the page. lib/scene.js and the bundled lib/three.js draw animated backgrounds, and lib/scene-media.js with the bundled Lottie player (lib/lottie/) plays a video or animation behind the page. reader.html opens a file the user imports, and reads only with a one-use pass from Glaze's worker. Chrome requires these packaged files to be web-accessible to load them into LMS pages.

### nativeMessaging

Optional and off by default. Requested only when the student turns on Settings → AI → Agents. It connects Glaze only to the glaze-mcp helper on their computer. Each app receives data only after approval for that app and data group: courses/to-dos/schedule, grades, assignments and rubrics, Writer documents, or mod tools. Every change, including a to-do tick, Writer fix or citation, waits for the student's OK. Agents cannot submit work. Glaze sends nothing to FrostDev. A receiving app may send the data to its own AI service under its own policy.

## Remote code (1,000 character maximum)

All of Glaze's own code ships in the package. The only code not in it is user-installed mods (Chrome only): JavaScript from a mod bundle the user chooses to install from a file, link, code or the Glaze gallery, or a signed update from the same author for a mod the user follows. Glaze never downloads code of its own to run. Before installing, the user sees what the mod may read and why, and approves it. Mod code runs only in the extension's manifest sandbox page, isolated from the LMS page, with no extension APIs and no cookies. Mods that read courses, to-dos or grades run in a Worker with no page of their own and reach the network only through Glaze, to HTTPS hosts that are declared and granted. A mod without data access may have a frame UI that can contact other sites. Updates must come signed from the same author and ask again for more access, and a signed blocklist can turn off harmful mods. Firefox and Safari builds run no mod code.

## Data usage (privacy practices tab)

Glaze has no analytics and does not sell user data or use it for unrelated purposes, creditworthiness or lending. Optional share sessions and mod channels send encrypted content to the selected relay. The optional signed blocklist sends a fixed credential-free GET without student identifiers. Optional AI sends a feature's context, such as a draft, to the provider or endpoint the user chooses; Writer's citation lookups send a DOI, ISBN or web address only on the user's click. With Agents on, the data groups the user allows go only to the AI apps they approve on their own computer, through the glaze-mcp helper; those apps may send it to their own AI services under their own policies, and Glaze sends nothing to FrostDev. User-installed mods and linked media may contact other hosts as described above; a mod granted personal data and network access can send that data to its granted hosts. These services receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address. Keep the store's data-use answers consistent with these behaviors and PRIVACY.md.
