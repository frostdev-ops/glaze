# Firefox listing privacy (en)

## Privacy

No analytics, no ads and no account. Settings stay in your browser and follow Firefox sync if it is on (they include course nicknames, GPA choices and some school/course/account identifiers). Course details, grade records, notes, tasks, your place, the due list and typing backups stay on your device. Glaze reads your own Blackboard, Canvas or Moodle site using your existing session, and writes to it only when you ask (a planner tick, a Canvas nickname or color, Sign out, Moodle's Edit mode). Chosen web fonts and background images make ordinary network requests; linked videos and animations are fetched by Glaze's worker without cookies, a referrer or redirects (12 MiB video, 6 MiB picture, 1 MiB Lottie; video/picture sides up to 4096 pixels). Identifying metadata is removed from the file’s container; encoded streams and visible pixels remain. Files saved before 3.3.5 that cannot be cleaned stay on this device and are not shared. Course tab pictures stay in memory and are never sent anywhere. Share sessions, only if you use them, send encrypted content that the relay cannot read. Glaze does not collect or sell your coursework, grades, credentials or browsing history. Full policy: https://github.com/frostdev-ops/glaze/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
