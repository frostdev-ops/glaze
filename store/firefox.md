# Firefox listing privacy (en)

## Privacy

No analytics, no ads and no account. Settings stay in your browser and follow Firefox sync if it is on (they include course nicknames, GPA choices and some school/course/account identifiers). Course details, grade records, notes, tasks, your place, the due list, typing backups and Writer's papers, documents and sources stay on your device. Glaze reads your own Blackboard, Canvas or Moodle site using your existing session, and writes to it only when you ask (a planner tick, a Canvas nickname or color, Sign out, Moodle's Edit mode, Writer's Post, Reply, Send or Submit, marking announcements read when opened in Canvas or in Moodle forums with automatic read tracking); previews and Blackboard send no read mark. Rubrics are read-only. AI is off by default. If you enable it for a feature, an on-device model works locally; a provider or endpoint you choose receives that feature's context, such as your draft and assignment materials. With AI Feedback on, assignment pictures can be read before Review; up to 128 checked readings stay locally for 30 days. AI outline sends sections while Outline is open; AI source extraction sends bibliography entries while Sources is open. Both have their own switches. Cite sends a DOI to doi.org, Crossref or DataCite, an ISBN to Open Library, or a web address to its own site, only when you click Look up. Chosen web fonts and background images make ordinary network requests; linked videos and animations are fetched by Glaze's worker without cookies, a referrer or redirects (12 MiB video, 6 MiB picture, 1 MiB Lottie; video/picture sides up to 4096 pixels). Identifying metadata is removed from the file’s container; encoded streams and visible pixels remain. Files saved before 3.3.5 that cannot be cleaned stay on this device and are not shared. Course tab sketches stay in memory and are never sent anywhere. Share sessions, only if you use them, send encrypted content that the relay cannot read. These services receive connection data such as your IP address. Glaze does not collect or sell your data. Full policy: https://github.com/frostdev-ops/glaze/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
